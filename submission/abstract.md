of the auxiliary linear order from every order-invariant cluster first-order
sentence. The graph domain may be empty. The concepts give the thesis's
cluster syntax, ordinary order-free syntax, their satisfaction relation,

ordered comparison lemma, the construction of orders from finitely many
first-order tests, and the final finite disjunction. The proof-dependency
